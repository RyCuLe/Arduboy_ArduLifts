# 🎲 ArduLifts

## About

Imagine the classic board game **Snakes & Ladders**, but with a board that changes every turn. That's **ArduLifts**!

Instead of fixed ladders, the board is filled with **lifts (or elevators, for our transatlantic friends!)**. At the start of every turn, each lift changes direction, meaning a shortcut one turn could become a setback the next. Every game is unpredictable.

Your goal is simple: be the first player to reach the finish tile. You **must land exactly on the final tile** to win, so if your roll would take you beyond the finish, you don't move.

---

## Rule Sets

### 🟩 Vanilla

The standard ArduLifts experience.

- Lifts are active.
- Every lift changes direction at the start of each turn.

### 🟦 No Lifts

A classic race to the finish.

- No lifts.
- No surprises.
- Just luck and the roll of the dice.

### 🟨 2nd Chance

Based on **Vanilla** rules.

After rolling the die, you may choose to roll again if you're unhappy with your result.

- You **must** accept the second roll, even if it's worse.

### 🟥 Extra Chaos

Based on **Vanilla** rules.

Whenever you roll a **6**, you don't move. Instead, you draw a **Chaos Card**.

Chaos Cards can have either beneficial or harmful effects, adding another layer of unpredictability to the game.
